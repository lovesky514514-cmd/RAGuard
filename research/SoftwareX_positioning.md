# SoftwareX positioning, novelty boundaries, and editorial checklist

**Recommended submission type:** Original Software Publication. **Draft only:** scope fit is not a promise of acceptance or current indexing status.

## Research software claim

RAGuard provides reusable *evaluation software* to expose disagreement between recovery-visible evidence and evaluator-only physical effects under uncertain external tool writes. Its differentiated operational components are stable business-identity handling, a persistent independent B5 baseline, local Go HTTP fault injection, budget-aware evidence experiments and traceable safety-adversarial tests.

| LIMBO, arXiv:2609.29095 | Duplicate effects, tool-contract conditions | Reusable Python/Go local evaluators and pinned paired evidence matrices | Substantial benchmark novelty beyond LIMBO not yet demonstrated |
| Conventional finite-world planning/POMDP | Budgeted queries and conditional decisions | CCEP included as exploratory tool, not claimed original | Same-cost meta-aware rule matches CCEP on reported slice |

**Forbidden claims:** novel exactly-once algorithm, demonstrated recovery superiority over B5, zero production risk, signed provider attestations, independent system replication, generalizable probability of zero unsafe outcomes.

## Scope decision

If the independent benchmark contribution cannot be distinguished from existing frameworks after full-text comparison, do not submit a paper claiming methodological novelty. The reusable software may still be a candidate, subject to SoftwareX editorial judgment. Software functioning alone does not guarantee acceptance.

## Suggested cover-letter claim (requires human approval)

*We submit RAGuard as an open research-software testbed for reproducing ambiguous external-write recovery in tool-using agents. We make a narrow, falsifiable contribution: a reproducible separation of physical side-effect observations from recovery-visible evidence, an independent durable baseline, and typed evidence audit tests. In our shared-safeguard comparison, B5 and RAGuard tie, and we report this negative result transparently. The software does not claim production exactly-once semantics.*
